Always included Layout, Advanced, Alerts: opens its own window

An Alerts window with one page per alert

Adding Alerts from Advanced puts one alert box on your canvas, which you move and size like any widget. A button on it opens the Alerts window.

  • Twitch: follows, subs and resubs, gifted subs, bits and raids.
  • YouTube: Super Chats, Super Stickers, new members and member milestones.
  • Streamlabs: donations (tips). You connect with your Streamlabs Socket API token, copied from the Streamlabs dashboard.
  • Per alert: text with {name}, {amount}, {months} and {message}, plus font, size, colour and outline. Add a picture, GIF or video. Choose the entry and exit animation, how long it stays, a sound with its volume, and where the picture sits (above, beside or behind the text).
  • Your own files: pick pictures, GIFs, videos and sounds from your PC. They're kept in Viperstrike's folder, so the overlay can play them.
  • Turn each alert on or off, with a Test button on every page.
  • Alerts take turns: a burst of follows plays one after another, never on top of each other.
